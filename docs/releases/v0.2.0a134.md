# BreadSched 0.2.0a134

Application version: `0.2.0a134`

Native schema version: `8`

Supported native schemas: `6–8`

This alpha introduces payees and a new native data format.

## Highlights

- A **payee** records who a transaction was with, separately from its
  description. Imported descriptions stay exactly as the statement printed them.
- `breadsched payees` adds a payee from example descriptions, previews which
  transactions without a payee match, and assigns only what you accept, in one
  undo step. Matching ignores case, punctuation, and words containing digits such
  as store or card numbers, and is otherwise exact.
- A transaction that already has a payee is never changed by a proposal, and
  re-importing from GnuCash, OFX, QIF, or CSV keeps the payees you assigned.

## Native-book compatibility

Native schema 8 adds a payee table. Books in schemas 6 and 7 are migrated
automatically the first time they are opened for writing, after a verified
pre-migration backup is written beside the book (for example
`book.breadsched.pre-migration-v7.bak`). Open an older book writable once to
migrate it; a read-only open of an older book is refused rather than migrated.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. After migration, a schema 8 book
cannot be opened by 0.2.0a133 or earlier; to roll back, restore the
pre-migration backup, which loses changes made since the upgrade.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes
a source distribution, pure-Python wheel, and `SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
