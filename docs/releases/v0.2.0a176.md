# BreadSched 0.2.0a176

Application version: `0.2.0a176`

Native schema version: `9`

Supported native schemas: `6–9`

This alpha holds what payers owe back in Receivable accounts (#170).

## Highlights

- Tracking a reimbursable expense moves what is owed from the expense into a
  Receivable account: net worth, never liquidity, Available, or the emergency fund.
- BreadSched keeps those reclassification transactions itself; money back and
  write-offs move the balance out again, and a dispute posts nothing.
- GnuCash A/Receivable accounts import as the new Receivable type.
- The Dashboard shows Reimbursements due, and a receivable warns when its expense
  is also on an FSA claim.

## Native-book compatibility

No schema change was made. Receivables gain an optional account and their own
reclassification transactions; a receivable recorded earlier posts them on its
next change. Schema 9 books open directly; schemas 6, 7, and 8
still migrate automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. This version does not migrate schema
9 books; 0.2.0a143 through 0.2.0a175 can reopen them.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes
a source distribution, pure-Python wheel, and `SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
