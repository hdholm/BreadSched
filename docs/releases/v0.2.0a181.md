# BreadSched 0.2.0a181

Application version: `0.2.0a181`

Native schema version: `9`

Supported native schemas: `6–9`

This alpha splits one bill between a payer and an FSA claim (#192).

## Highlights

- On an FSA claim, **Payer covers part** names the receivable whose payer covers
  part of the same bill; both then show the payer's, the FSA's, and your share.
- The FSA share stays zero until the EOB responsibility is entered; a payer and
  EOB that together exceed what was paid read **Needs review**, never a refusal.

## Native-book compatibility

No data-format change was made. Schema 9 books open directly; schemas 6, 7, and 8
still migrate automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. This version does not migrate schema
9 books; 0.2.0a143 through 0.2.0a180 can reopen them.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes
a source distribution, pure-Python wheel, and `SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
