# BreadSched 0.2.0a191

Application version: `0.2.0a191`

Native schema version: `9`

Supported native schemas: `6–9`

This alpha explains a net worth change by the postings that made it.

## Highlights

- Choose a period's change in Net worth history (desktop, browser, or the new
  `breadsched net-worth-change` command) to list the transactions that changed net
  worth, with each effect converted at its own date's quote. A **Market and
  exchange-rate changes** line reconciles them to the change exactly.
- Transfers between your own accounts are counted and left out; a posting without a
  quote withholds the totals and is named.
- The printout and CSV export carry the same totals as the screen.

## Native-book compatibility

No data-format change was made. Schema 9 books open directly; schemas 6, 7, and 8
still migrate automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. This version does not migrate schema
9 books; 0.2.0a143 through 0.2.0a190 can reopen them.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes
a source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, and `SHA256SUMS` for the
tested commit.
