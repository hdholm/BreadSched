# BreadSched 0.2.0a196

Application version: `0.2.0a196`

Native schema version: `10`

Supported native schemas: `6–10`

This alpha records the AqBanking decision and documents bank downloads through it.

## Highlights

- BreadSched does not connect to banks itself or bundle AqBanking. AqBanking mainly
  serves German and other European banks; its US route, OFX Direct Connect, is
  being withdrawn by major banks.
- If your bank works with AqBanking, the User Guide shows how to download a
  statement with `aqbanking-cli`, export it as CSV, and import it with the
  reviewed CSV import. Downloading overlapping days again adds nothing twice.

## Native-book compatibility

No data-format change was made. Schema 10 books open directly; schemas 6, 7, 8,
and 9 still migrate automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. This version does not migrate schema
10 books, and 0.2.0a195 reopens them unchanged. Alphas before 0.2.0a193 cannot
open a schema 10 book; restore its pre-migration backup to go back.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes
a source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, and `SHA256SUMS` for the
tested commit.
