# BreadSched 0.2.0a197

Application version: `0.2.0a197`

Native schema version: `10`

Supported native schemas: `6–10`

This alpha holds GnuCash deletions of reconciled transactions for review.

## Highlights

- A transaction deleted in GnuCash is no longer removed silently when any of its
  splits is reconciled, in GnuCash or in BreadSched. It joins the review of held
  GnuCash changes, where you can keep it, delete it here too, or decide later.
  Deleting is refused while a reconciliation, FSA claim, or receivable refers to it.
- OFX and QIF investment records that are not imported, such as stock splits,
  share transfers, and option activity, are now reported with their date and a
  plain reason, and the User Guide lists every such kind.

## Native-book compatibility

No data-format change was made; held deletions are stored with the existing
review records. Schema 10 books open directly; schemas 6, 7, 8, and 9 still
migrate automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. This version does not migrate schema
10 books. 0.2.0a196 reopens them; it removes a held deletion's transaction on its
next GnuCash import and ignores the held entry. Alphas before 0.2.0a193 cannot
open a schema 10 book; restore its pre-migration backup to go back.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes
a source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, and `SHA256SUMS` for the
tested commit.
