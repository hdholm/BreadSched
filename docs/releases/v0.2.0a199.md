# BreadSched 0.2.0a199

Application version: `0.2.0a199`

Native schema version: `10`

Supported native schemas: `6–10`

This alpha lets several scenarios' projections stay open as tabs, and remembers
each book's tabs.

## Highlights

- On the desktop, **New tab** in Projection keeps the scenario shown in a tab of
  its own. The main Projection tab still follows Plan's scenario.
- Reopening a book brings back its views, registers, and scenario tabs.
- In the browser, **Open in new tab** in Register and Projection opens that
  account or scenario in another browser tab.

## Native-book compatibility

No data-format change was made. Schema 10 books open directly; schemas 6, 7, 8,
and 9 still migrate automatically with a verified backup. Remembered tabs live in
the per-user `views.ini`, not in the book.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. This version does not migrate schema 10
books, and 0.2.0a198 reopens them unchanged, ignoring the remembered tabs. Alphas
before 0.2.0a193 cannot open a schema 10 book; restore its pre-migration backup to
go back.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes
a source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, and `SHA256SUMS` for the
tested commit.
