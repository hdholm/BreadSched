# BreadSched 0.2.0a204

Application version: `0.2.0a204`

Native schema version: `10`

Supported native schemas: `6–10`

This alpha flags the FSA claims that need attention and totals claims by status,
account, funding year, or provider.

## Highlights

- The Dashboard counts FSA claims needing attention: figures that need review, no
  EOB after 30 days, a claim deadline within 30 days, or a rejected reimbursement.
- The FSA Dashboard says why each claim needs attention and has a claims report
  grouped by status, FSA account, funding year, or provider.
- `breadsched claims` prints the report from the command line.

## Native-book compatibility

No data-format change was made. Schema 10 books open directly; schemas 6, 7, 8,
and 9 still migrate automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. This version does not migrate schema 10
books, and 0.2.0a203 reopens them unchanged. Alphas before 0.2.0a193 cannot open a
schema 10 book; restore its pre-migration backup to go back.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes
a source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, and `SHA256SUMS` for the
tested commit.
