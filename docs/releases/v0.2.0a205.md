# BreadSched 0.2.0a205

Application version: `0.2.0a205`

Native schema version: `10`

Supported native schemas: `6–10`

This alpha handles FSA claims that change after they are filed: over-reimbursements
repaid to the FSA, late EOB changes, and closing or reopening a claim.

## Highlights

- A lower EOB after reimbursement shows the claim as **Over-reimbursed**; linking
  the repayment into the FSA settles it and gives the funding year's election back.
- Each EOB change, closing, and reopening is kept in the claim's history, and a
  higher EOB reopens a reimbursed claim for the rest.
- **Close claim** records what is given up; **Reopen** takes the claim up again.
  `breadsched claims --close`, `--reopen`, and `--history` do the same.

## Native-book compatibility

No data-format change was made. Schema 10 books open directly; schemas 6, 7, 8,
and 9 still migrate automatically with a verified backup. Repayments, a claim's
history, and its closing are stored with the claim.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. This version does not migrate schema 10
books, and 0.2.0a204 reopens them unchanged, but saving a claim there drops its
repayment links, history, and closing, and it counts a repayment into the FSA as
funding. Alphas before 0.2.0a193 cannot open a schema 10 book; restore its
pre-migration backup to go back.
