# BreadSched 0.2.0a206

Application version: `0.2.0a206`

Native schema version: `10`

Supported native schemas: `6–10`

This alpha follows an FSA plan's rule for unused money: a carryover into the next
plan year, or a grace period for claiming later services.

## Highlights

- A funding year's carryover limit moves up to that much unused election into the
  next funding year once the run-out ends; only the rest is forfeited.
- A grace period lets services after the plan year be claimed against it.
- The FSA Dashboard shows what each year carried in and carried over.

## Native-book compatibility

No data-format change was made. Schema 10 books open directly; schemas 6, 7, 8,
and 9 still migrate automatically with a verified backup. The plan rules are stored
with the FSA account's funding years.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. This version does not migrate schema 10
books, and 0.2.0a205 reopens them unchanged, but it ignores the plan rules, and
saving the FSA account there drops them. Alphas before 0.2.0a193 cannot open a
schema 10 book; restore its pre-migration backup to go back.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes
a source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, and `SHA256SUMS` for the
tested commit.
