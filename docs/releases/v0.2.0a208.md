# BreadSched 0.2.0a208

Application version: `0.2.0a208`

Native schema version: `10`

Supported native schemas: `6–10`

This alpha gives each reporting term one meaning across Plan, Expense Explorer,
the command line, and printed reports (#235).

## Highlights

- The Plan summary's variance now compares actual and planned amounts through the
  same as-of date. A transaction entered ahead for later in the current month no
  longer appears in the variance while being left out of the actual beside it.
- Period columns are labelled **Period actual** and **Period variance**: they
  include everything dated in the period, and a transaction's detail says when it
  is dated after the as-of date.
- The browser, printed Plan, and `breadsched activity` show the planned change
  through the as-of date, and the User Guide defines each term with an example.

## Native-book compatibility

No data-format change was made. Schema 10 books open directly; schemas 6, 7, 8,
and 9 still migrate automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. This version does not migrate schema 10
books, and 0.2.0a207 reopens them unchanged. Alphas before 0.2.0a193 cannot open a
schema 10 book; restore its pre-migration backup to go back.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes
a source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, and `SHA256SUMS` for the
tested commit.
