# BreadSched 0.2.0a210

Application version: `0.2.0a210`

Native schema version: `10`

Supported native schemas: `6–10`

This alpha counts each way FSA money moves exactly once, and brings `DESIGN.md` and
`CHANGELOG.md` in line with the implemented application (#237).

## Highlights

- FSA money moves are classified as funding, a direct payment from the FSA card, a
  reimbursement to your bank, a provider refund to the card, a repayment, or a
  transfer. Only funding counts as funded; direct payments and reimbursements use
  the election, and provider refunds give it back.
- Correction: a provider refund credited to the FSA card was counted as payroll
  funding and did not restore the election.
- Plan shows one net **Benefit funding** row per FSA account, so an expense the FSA
  paid no longer leaves an unexplained residual in the cash bridge. The medical
  expense is still counted once, however it was paid.
- Linking a transaction to a claim offers **Paid from the FSA card** and **Refunded
  to the FSA card**, which record both sides of a card payment or refund at once.
- The FSA Dashboard in the desktop and browser adds a **How used** column, and
  `breadsched claims --years` lists benefit years with the same breakdown.
- `DESIGN.md` is reorganized to describe the implemented architecture, and older
  `CHANGELOG.md` entries are condensed with links to their release notes.

## Native-book compatibility

No data-format change was made. Schema 10 books open directly; schemas 6, 7, 8,
and 9 still migrate automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. This version does not migrate schema 10
books, and 0.2.0a209 reopens them unchanged. A paired role is stored as the two
existing links (a payment and a reimbursement, or a refund and a repayment), so
0.2.0a209 reads such claims correctly, though it reports FSA usage the old way. Alphas before
0.2.0a193 cannot open a schema 10 book; restore its pre-migration backup to go back.

The web FSA Dashboard's benefit years gain `direct_payments`, `reimbursements`,
`provider_refunds`, `repaid`, and `usage_text`, and Review's FSA role choices carry
a `label`. Existing fields are unchanged.
