# BreadSched 0.2.0a215

Application version: `0.2.0a215`

Native schema version: `10`

Supported native schemas: `6–10`

This alpha shows how every projected balance reconciles.

## Highlights

- For any projected month, and across the whole projection, BreadSched shows four
  bridges: **Cash**, **Investments**, **Debts**, and **Net worth**. Each starts at the
  opening balance, adds the planned events and the interest, performance, or debt
  interest the assumptions produce, and reaches the closing balance.
- Net worth takes planned events net of transfers, so a contribution or a principal
  payment moves money between balances without changing net worth.
- Each bridge ends with **Unexplained**, always zero: the projection already refuses a
  month its terms do not explain.
- In the desktop application, the month explanation adds **How this month reconciles**
  and **Across the whole projection**. In the browser, the same appear in the month
  dialog and under the Projection chart. `breadsched project --bridge` prints them
  for the whole projection, and `--bridge YYYY-MM` for one month.

## Native-book compatibility

No data-format change was made. Schema 10 books open directly; schemas 6, 7, 8,
and 9 still migrate automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. This version does not migrate schema 10
books, and 0.2.0a214 reopens them unchanged. Alphas before 0.2.0a193 cannot open a
schema 10 book; restore its pre-migration backup to go back.

The web projection response and the month explanation gain `bridges`; `breadsched
project --json --bridge` gains `bridge_period` and `bridges`. Existing fields are
unchanged.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes
a source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, and
`SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
