# BreadSched 0.2.0a221

Application version: `0.2.0a221`

Native schema version: `10`

Supported native schemas: `6–10`

This alpha checks savings goals kept in investment and other non-cash accounts in
the Projection.

## Highlights

- A savings goal held in a brokerage or other non-cash account now gets a verdict in
  the Projection: whether that account's projected balance in the target month
  covers everything goals in that account have set aside. Before, such a goal was
  listed without a comparison. A goal in an account left out of projections is still
  listed without one, and says why.

## Native-book compatibility

No data-format change was made. Schema 10 books open directly; schemas 6, 7, 8,
and 9 still migrate automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. This version does not migrate schema 10
books, and 0.2.0a220 reopens them unchanged. Alphas before 0.2.0a193 cannot open a
schema 10 book; restore its pre-migration backup to go back.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes
a source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, and
`SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
