# BreadSched 0.2.0a223

Application version: `0.2.0a223`

Native schema version: `10`

Supported native schemas: `6–10`

This alpha lets a scenario spend a savings goal on what it was saved for.

## Highlights

- In a scenario, a savings goal can now include its purchase: a date on or after the
  target date and the expense or asset account it buys into. That scenario's Plan
  and Projection show the purchase as a one-off out of the goal's account, the goal
  stops setting money aside from then, and the Projection note says when it is
  bought. Base and every other scenario are unchanged. Use **Purchase in that
  scenario** in the desktop goals window, **Buy on** and **Buy into** in the
  browser, or `breadsched goals --override … --buy-on … --buy-into …`.

## Native-book compatibility

The purchase is stored inside the scenario's existing goal change; no schema
change was made. Schema 10 books open directly; schemas 6, 7, 8, and 9 still
migrate automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. This version does not migrate schema 10
books. 0.2.0a222 reopens them, but ignores and, on the next save of that scenario,
drops a modelled purchase. Alphas before 0.2.0a193 cannot open a schema 10 book;
restore its pre-migration backup to go back.

## Verified artifacts
