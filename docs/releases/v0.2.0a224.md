# BreadSched 0.2.0a224

Application version: `0.2.0a224`

Native schema version: `10`

Supported native schemas: `6–10`

This alpha supports dependent care FSAs.

## Highlights

- Mark an FSA as a **dependent care FSA** in its account settings. BreadSched then
  follows that plan's rules: only what has been contributed so far is available, a
  claim needs no EOB, a claim larger than the contributions stays open until more
  arrive, and nothing carries over into the next plan year.
- In the browser, a refused change to an account's FSA years, type, emergency-fund
  setting, or card payment no longer leaves the open book showing the refused value.

## Native-book compatibility

The dependent care mark is stored with the account; no schema change was made.
Schema 10 books open directly; schemas 6, 7, 8, and 9 still migrate automatically
with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. This version does not migrate schema 10
books. 0.2.0a223 reopens them but treats a dependent care FSA as a health care FSA,
and drops the mark the next time that account is saved. Alphas before 0.2.0a193
cannot open a schema 10 book; restore its pre-migration backup to go back.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes
a source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, and
`SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
