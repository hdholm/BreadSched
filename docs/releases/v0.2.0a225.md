# BreadSched 0.2.0a225

Application version: `0.2.0a225`

Native schema version: `10`

Supported native schemas: `6–10`

This alpha counts expected reimbursements in the Plan and Projection.

## Highlights

- A reimbursable expense with an **expected by** date now appears in the Plan and
  Projection as money arriving that day, in the account that paid the expense.
  Projected cash rises only from then; net worth does not change, because the
  money was already counted as owed to you.
- A disputed reimbursement, one past its expected date, and one settled or written
  off are not counted on, so doubtful money never shows as spendable cash.

## Native-book compatibility

No data-format change was made. Schema 10 books open directly; schemas 6, 7, 8,
and 9 still migrate automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. This version does not migrate schema 10
books, and 0.2.0a224 reopens them unchanged. Alphas before 0.2.0a193 cannot open a
schema 10 book; restore its pre-migration backup to go back.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes
a source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, and
`SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
