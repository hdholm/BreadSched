# BreadSched 0.2.0a231

Application version: `0.2.0a231`

Native schema version: `10`

Supported native schemas: `6–10`

This alpha reorganizes the historical-estimate and Dashboard bill code; nothing you
see changes.

## Highlights

- The statistics behind **Suggest** for estimates from history (how regular a
  category's spending is, its typical amount, trend, and seasonality) now live in
  their own module, separate from the code that reads your book. The suggestions
  are unchanged.
- The Dashboard's bill rows, reserves, and liquidity figures are computed in their
  own module, apart from the account groups. The Dashboard is unchanged.

## Native-book compatibility

No data-format change was made. Schema 10 books open directly; schemas 6, 7, 8,
and 9 still migrate automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. This version does not migrate schema 10
books, and 0.2.0a230 reopens them unchanged. Alphas before 0.2.0a193 cannot open a
schema 10 book; restore its pre-migration backup to go back.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes
a source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, and
`SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
