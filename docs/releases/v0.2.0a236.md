# BreadSched 0.2.0a236

Application version: `0.2.0a236`

Native schema version: `10`

Supported native schemas: `6–10`

This alpha lets you set up retirement drawdowns on the desktop and in the browser.

## Highlights

- In the desktop scenario manager, **Edit retirement drawdowns…** lists a saved
  scenario's drawdowns and adds, edits, or removes them. The browser's Manage
  scenarios page has the same **Retirement drawdowns** section. Choose the account to
  withdraw from, the cash account to pay into, the dates, and either a fixed yearly
  amount or a yearly share of the balance.

## Native-book compatibility

No data-format change was made. Schema 10 books open directly; schemas 6, 7, 8,
and 9 still migrate automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. This version does not migrate schema 10
books, and 0.2.0a235 reopens them unchanged. Alphas before 0.2.0a193 cannot open a
schema 10 book; restore its pre-migration backup to go back.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes
a source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, and
`SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
