# BreadSched 0.2.0a237

Application version: `0.2.0a237`

Native schema version: `10`

Supported native schemas: `6–10`

This alpha shows the gross and net cost of expected reimbursements in Projection
and lets a scenario plan for a payer paying less, nothing, or later.

## Highlights

- Projection lists each reimbursement still expected in its range: what was spent,
  what has already come back, what the scenario expects and when, and your net cost.
- A scenario can expect less (or nothing) back, or on another date. The part it does
  not expect is projected as written off, back into the expense, so that scenario's
  spending, cash, and net worth show the difference while others are unchanged. Use
  `breadsched receivables --scenario NAME --expect RECEIVABLE`; desktop and browser
  editors follow.

## Native-book compatibility

No data-format change was made. A scenario's reimbursement changes are stored in
the scenario; an older version ignores them and drops them if it saves that
scenario. Schema 10 books open directly; schemas 6, 7, 8, and 9 still migrate
automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. This version does not migrate schema 10
books, and 0.2.0a236 reopens them unchanged. Alphas before 0.2.0a193 cannot open a
schema 10 book; restore its pre-migration backup to go back.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes
a source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, and
`SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
