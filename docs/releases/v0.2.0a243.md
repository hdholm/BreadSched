# BreadSched 0.2.0a243

Application version: `0.2.0a243`

Native schema version: `10`

Supported native schemas: `6–10`

This alpha says how long your cash lasts in every projection and comparison.

## Highlights

- Each projection now states whether cash lasts the whole projection or the month
  it runs out and how many months it covers, the lowest cash and when, and when a
  retirement drawdown empties its account.
- Comparing two scenarios says which one's cash lasts longer and by how much, on
  the desktop, in the browser, in print, and on the command line.

## Native-book compatibility

No data-format change was made. Schema 10 books open directly; schemas 6, 7, 8,
and 9 still migrate automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. This version does not migrate schema 10
books, and 0.2.0a242 reopens them unchanged. Alphas before 0.2.0a193 cannot open a
schema 10 book; restore its pre-migration backup to go back.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes
a source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, and
`SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
