# BreadSched 0.2.0a244

Application version: `0.2.0a244`

Native schema version: `10`

Supported native schemas: `6–10`

This alpha shows what your investments cost and what you have gained.

## Highlights

- **Holdings and cost basis** lists each security with its shares, cost, market
  value, and unrealized gain or loss, and each holding's lots and sales with the
  gain each sale realized, totalled by year. Sales take the oldest shares first.
- When a cost cannot be known exactly (shares that arrived with no cost, sales
  beyond the recorded purchases, a missing price) BreadSched says so instead of
  guessing. Use it from the desktop or browser Accounts view, or `breadsched
  holdings`.

## Native-book compatibility

No data-format change was made. Schema 10 books open directly; schemas 6, 7, 8,
and 9 still migrate automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. This version does not migrate schema 10
books, and 0.2.0a243 reopens them unchanged. Alphas before 0.2.0a193 cannot open a
schema 10 book; restore its pre-migration backup to go back.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes
a source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, and
`SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
