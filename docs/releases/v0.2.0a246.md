# BreadSched 0.2.0a246

Application version: `0.2.0a246`

Native schema version: `10`

Supported native schemas: `6–10`

This alpha lets an investment account use average cost for the shares it sells, and
keeps a dependent-care FSA marked as such when a GnuCash book is re-imported.

## Highlights

- **Average cost.** Set an Investment or Retirement account's **Cost of shares
  sold** to Average cost (desktop account editor, browser account settings, or
  `breadsched account BOOK edit --name ACCOUNT --cost-basis average`). Each share
  sold then costs the average of every share held; Holdings and cost basis
  recalculates past sales and says which method it used. First in, first out
  remains the default.
- **Re-import fix.** Re-importing a GnuCash book used to clear the dependent-care
  mark on a Flexible Spending Account. It is now kept, like the account's plan
  years and its cost basis method.

## Native-book compatibility

No data-format change was made. The method is stored with the account's other
settings; accounts without it use first in, first out. Schema 10 books open
directly; schemas 6, 7, 8, and 9 still migrate automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. This version does not migrate schema 10
books, and 0.2.0a245 reopens them unchanged, using first in, first out for every
account. Alphas before 0.2.0a193 cannot open a schema 10 book; restore its
pre-migration backup to go back.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes

`SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
