# BreadSched 0.2.0a247

Application version: `0.2.0a247`

Native schema version: `10`

Supported native schemas: `6–10`

This alpha keeps cost basis correct when shares move between your accounts or a
security splits.

## Highlights

- **Transfers keep their cost.** Moving shares from one of your Investment or
  Retirement accounts to another is no longer counted as a sale. The shares keep
  their purchase dates and cost, nothing is realized, and later sales from the
  receiving account use that cost.
- **Share splits.** A transaction that changes the number of shares with no value
  on any leg is treated as a split: every lot holds proportionally more (or fewer)
  shares at the same cost. Previously the new shares counted at zero cost, and a
  reverse split counted as a sale at a loss.
- **Shown everywhere.** Holdings and cost basis lists each transfer and split with
  the lots and sales, on the desktop, in the browser, and in `breadsched holdings
  --lots`.
- **Known gap.** A GnuCash stock split recorded as a single split is still skipped
  on import and named in the import report; enter it in BreadSched instead.

## Native-book compatibility

No data-format change was made. Schema 10 books open directly; schemas 6, 7, 8,
and 9 still migrate automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. This version does not migrate schema 10
books, and 0.2.0a246 reopens them unchanged, treating transfers as sales again.
Alphas before 0.2.0a193 cannot open a schema 10 book; restore its pre-migration
backup to go back.
