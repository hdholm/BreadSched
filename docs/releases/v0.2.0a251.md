# BreadSched 0.2.0a251

Application version: `0.2.0a251`

Native schema version: `11`

Supported native schemas: `6–11`

This alpha makes typing into a register quicker. The desktop and browser registers
read dates, amounts, accounts, and check numbers by the same rules.

## Highlights

- **Dates.** Type `15` for that day of the month, `3/15` for that day this year, or
  a full date. While the field holds a date, `+` and `-` move a day, `]` and `[` a
  month, and `t` jumps to today; `m`/`h` and `y`/`r` jump to the start and end of
  the month and year.
- **Amounts.** Type arithmetic such as `12.50+3*2` or `100/3`; leaving the field
  shows the result, rounded to the cent. A negative result moves to the other
  column.
- **Accounts.** Type a path such as `Ex:Gr` into the Transfer choice or a split
  line's account to pick Expenses:Groceries.
- **Numbers.** `+` and `-` in Num step a check number; in an empty field they
  continue from the register's last one.
- The browser register's date is now a text field, so these keys can be typed.

## Native-book compatibility

No schema change. Schema 11 books open directly; schemas 6 through 10 migrate
automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. Going back to 0.2.0a250 needs no
restore; earlier alphas cannot reopen a schema 11 book.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes
a source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, and
`SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
