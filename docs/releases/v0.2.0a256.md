# BreadSched 0.2.0a256

Application version: `0.2.0a256`

Native schema version: `11`

Supported native schemas: `6–11`

This alpha fetches online prices and exchange rates from the command line.

## Highlights

- **Online quotes.** Give a commodity a quote source and run `breadsched quotes`:
  - `tsp`: Thrift Savings Plan fund prices from tsp.gov.
  - `currency`: exchange rates from the European Central Bank.
  - `alphavantage`: stock and fund prices from Alpha Vantage, with your free API key.
  - Any other Finance::Quote source, when Finance::Quote is installed (not in the
    Flatpak).

  Prices are stored with their source (for example *Online: tsp.gov*) and date.
  Fetching again the same day replaces that day's online price, and a failed source
  is listed with its reason without changing anything.
- **Quote sources from GnuCash.** Importing a GnuCash book now brings over the
  quote source of each commodity GnuCash fetches prices for.
- `breadsched quote-source` sets or clears a commodity's source; `breadsched quotes
  --list` shows what would be fetched and whether Finance::Quote is available.
  Getting quotes from the desktop and the browser comes next.

## Native-book compatibility

No schema change: a commodity stores its quote source only when it has one. Schema
11 books open directly; schemas 6 through 10 migrate automatically with a verified
backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. Re-import a GnuCash book once to pick up
its quote sources. Going back to 0.2.0a255 ignores quote sources and keeps every
fetched price; earlier alphas cannot reopen a schema 11 book.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes
a source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, and
`SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
