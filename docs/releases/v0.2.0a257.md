# BreadSched 0.2.0a257

Application version: `0.2.0a257`

Native schema version: `11`

Supported native schemas: `6–11`

This alpha fetches online prices from the desktop and the browser.

## Highlights

- **Online quotes.** In Accounts, choose **Online Quotes…** (desktop) or **Online
  quotes…** (browser) to see each security and foreign currency with its quote
  source. Change sources (`tsp`, `alphavantage`, `currency`, or a Finance::Quote
  method), enter your Alpha Vantage key, and choose **Get quotes**. The dialog lists
  each stored price with its source and date, and each failure with its reason.
- The desktop fetches in the background, so the window stays responsive.
- Your Alpha Vantage key is stored in your own settings file, readable only by you,
  and never in the book or sent back to the browser.
- Prices now show every digit they have (18.4521, not 18.45), here and in
  `breadsched quotes`.

## Native-book compatibility

No schema change. Schema 11 books open directly; schemas 6 through 10 migrate
automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. Going back to 0.2.0a256 needs no
restore; earlier alphas cannot reopen a schema 11 book.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes
a source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, and
`SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
