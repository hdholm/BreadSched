# BreadSched 0.2.0a263

Application version: `0.2.0a263`

Native schema version: `11`

Supported native schemas: `6–11`

This alpha fixes a frozen start-up when an upgraded book also had schedules due.

## Highlights

- **One start-up dialog at a time.** Upgrading a book that also had schedules due
  opened the upgrade notice and the due review together. The notice, hidden behind
  the review, took every input, so the review's buttons did nothing and the main
  window stayed greyed out. Now the notice comes first; the GnuCash change review
  and the due review follow, each after the one before it closes.
- If you hit this in an earlier alpha: move the due review aside to uncover the
  notice behind it and close the notice; the review then responds.

## Native-book compatibility

No schema change. Schema 11 books open directly; schemas 6 through 10 migrate
automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. Going back to 0.2.0a262 needs no
restore; earlier alphas cannot reopen a schema 11 book.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes a
source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, Debian
and Fedora packages, and `SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
