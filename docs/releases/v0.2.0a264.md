# BreadSched 0.2.0a264

Application version: `0.2.0a264`

Native schema version: `11`

Supported native schemas: `6–11`

This alpha lets a sale name the lots it sells and adds a realized-gains report.

## Highlights

- **Choose the lots a sale sells.** In Holdings and Cost Basis, **Choose Lots…**
  beside a sale lists the lots held just before it. Enter the shares to take from
  each; any shares left over are sold by the account's first-in, first-out or
  average-cost method, and clearing the choice returns the sale to that method. The
  choice survives editing the sale and re-importing it from GnuCash, and is not
  written back to GnuCash.
- **Realized gains.** A report of every sale with its proceeds, the cost of the
  lots it took, and the gain, with totals by year (per currency). Pick a year and
  print it from the desktop or the browser.
- On the command line: `breadsched sale-lots` and `breadsched realized-gains`.

## Native-book compatibility

No schema change. Schema 11 books open directly; schemas 6 through 10 migrate
automatically with a verified backup. A sale's named lots are stored in the sale's
split and only when you name them.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. Going back to 0.2.0a263 needs no
restore, but that version ignores named lots: it sells those shares by the
account's method, and editing such a sale there discards the choice.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes a
