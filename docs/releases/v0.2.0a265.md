# BreadSched 0.2.0a265

Application version: `0.2.0a265`

Native schema version: `11`

Supported native schemas: `6–11`

This alpha adds a Tax Year report: what a calendar year's US return draws on from
your book.

## Highlights

- **Realized gains by term.** Each sale is split into short-term and long-term
  parts: a lot is long-term when sold more than a year after its purchase. Each line
  shows the security, shares, purchase date (or *Various*), sale date, proceeds,
  cost, and gain, with totals per term.
- **Tax-relevant accounts and tags.** Mark the accounts (they count with the
  accounts beneath them) and tags the year should total. Accounts GnuCash marks
  *tax related* start out marked; your own choice is kept when you re-import and is
  not written back to GnuCash.
- **Income by source.** Every income account's total for the year.
- Open it from Accounts in the desktop (**Actions → Tax Year…**) or the browser
  (**Tax year…**), print it from either, or use `breadsched tax-year` and
  `breadsched tax-marks`.
- It is a summary of your records, not tax advice or a tax form.

## Native-book compatibility

No schema change. Schema 11 books open directly; schemas 6 through 10 migrate
automatically with a verified backup. An account's tax mark is stored only when
you set one, and the marked tags in book metadata.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. Going back to 0.2.0a264 needs no
restore; that version has no Tax Year report and ignores the marks, and editing a
marked account there drops that account's mark.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes a
source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, Debian
and Fedora packages, and `SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
