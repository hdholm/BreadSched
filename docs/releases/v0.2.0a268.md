# BreadSched 0.2.0a268

Application version: `0.2.0a268`

Native schema version: `11`

Supported native schemas: `6–11`

This alpha moves the Projection chart onto the shared charts.

## Highlights

- **A clearer Projection chart.** Cash, investments, and net worth by month, with
  the first month cash runs short marked (or the month savings goals' set-aside
  money first exceeds cash). Comparing with another scenario adds its net worth as
  a fourth line, now in the browser as well as the desktop.
- **Exact amounts.** Hover over the chart to see every line's amount for a month.
  In the browser, **Chart values** lists every month; printing can include them as
  the optional **Projection chart values** section.
- Months that leave out an amount in a currency without an exchange rate are shaded
  and named under the chart, as before.

## Native-book compatibility

No schema change. Schema 11 books open directly; schemas 6 through 10 migrate
automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. Going back to 0.2.0a267 needs no
restore; that version draws the older Projection chart.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes a
source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, Debian
and Fedora packages, and `SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
