# BreadSched 0.2.0a270

Application version: `0.2.0a270`

Native schema version: `11`

Supported native schemas: `6–11`

This alpha charts the Expense Explorer's spending and income on BreadSched's
shared charts.

## Highlights

- **Spending by category over time.** Spending over time now shows three charts:
  total plan against actual, actual stacked by your top-level expense categories,
  and each category's share of the period's actual. The seven largest categories
  are named and the rest are combined as Other, so every column still adds up to
  the period's actual. Income over time gets the same three charts.
- **Choose a period from any chart.** Click a period in any of these charts, or in
  the browser Tab to it and press Enter, to make it the comparison period.
- **Category trend chart.** The selected category's plan and actual across the
  Plan range are charted beside a table of its values.
- **Exact values everywhere.** Hovering shows exact amounts and shares; the browser
  keeps each chart's values under a Chart values toggle, and printing puts the
  charts beside their tables.

## Native-book compatibility

No schema change. Schema 11 books open directly; schemas 6 through 10 migrate
automatically with a verified backup. Charts are drawn from the book and never
stored.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. Going back to 0.2.0a269 needs no
restore; that version draws the Expense Explorer's earlier charts.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes a
source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, Debian
and Fedora packages, and `SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
