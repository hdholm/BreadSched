# BreadSched 0.2.0a271

Application version: `0.2.0a271`

Native schema version: `11`

Supported native schemas: `6–11`

This alpha charts Net worth history, with what makes it up, on BreadSched's shared
charts.

## Highlights

- **What your net worth is made of.** A new chart stacks each group of accounts
  for every period: assets above zero and debts below, so each column adds up to
  that period's net worth. When all your assets sit under one **Assets** account,
  its subaccounts are the groups, such as checking, savings, and investments.
- **Assets, debts, and net worth over time.** The line chart now draws all
  three, and marks the period valued to date.
- **Missing quotes stay visible.** A period that needs a quote that did not exist
  is left out of the charts, and the charts say which period, rather than
  drawing it as zero.
- **Printed and in the browser too.** The browser Dashboard and the printout show
  both charts, with each chart's exact values beside it, and each period lists
  its groups.

## Native-book compatibility

No schema change. Schema 11 books open directly; schemas 6 through 10 migrate
automatically with a verified backup. Charts are drawn from the book and never
stored.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. Going back to 0.2.0a270 needs no
restore; that version draws the earlier net worth chart.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes a
source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, Debian
and Fedora packages, and `SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
