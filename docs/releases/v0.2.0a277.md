# BreadSched 0.2.0a277

Application version: `0.2.0a277`

Native schema version: `11`

Supported native schemas: `6–11`

This alpha makes the Expense Explorer fast and readable (#310, #311).

## Highlights

- **Much faster.** On a book with about 17,000 transactions the Expense Explorer
  took roughly 85 seconds to open on the desktop and over 40 seconds for each
  change of period or category. It now opens in about three seconds, and changing
  a choice is immediate. The browser's explorer and its printout are faster too.
- **Never looks frozen.** The desktop window opens at once and says it is
  calculating; **Cancel** closes it.
- **Readable choices.** Period, Sort categories, Category trend, and Income detail
  now sit two to a row with room for their text, and Print and Close moved to the
  bottom of the window.

## Native-book compatibility

No schema change. Schema 11 books open directly; schemas 6 through 10 migrate
automatically with a verified backup. The faster lookup reads the book with
SQLite's JSON functions when available and otherwise as before.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. Going back to 0.2.0a276 needs no
restore; that version has the slow Expense Explorer.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes a
source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, Debian
and Fedora packages, and `SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
