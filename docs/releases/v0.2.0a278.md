# BreadSched 0.2.0a278

Application version: `0.2.0a278`

Native schema version: `11`

Supported native schemas: `6–11`

This alpha makes register entry fast, adds notes to the register, and makes
scrollbars visible (#319, #318).

## Highlights

- **Fast entry.** Saving a transaction in a long register took several seconds and
  could make the desktop say BreadSched was not responding. It now takes a fraction
  of a second: the register reloads only what changed, and views you are not looking
  at update when you next show them.
- **Notes in the register.** Choose **Double Line** in the register's toolbar to see
  and type each transaction's notes on a second line, including for a new entry.
  BreadSched remembers the choice.
- **Visible scrollbars.** Lists that are longer than their window, such as the due
  review, now always show a scrollbar.

## Native-book compatibility

No schema change. Schema 11 books open directly; schemas 6 through 10 migrate
automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. Going back to 0.2.0a277 needs no
restore; that version keeps notes typed here but cannot show them in the register.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes a
source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, Debian
and Fedora packages, and `SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
