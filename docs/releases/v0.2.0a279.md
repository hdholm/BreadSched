# BreadSched 0.2.0a279

Application version: `0.2.0a279`

Native schema version: `11`

Supported native schemas: `6–11`

This alpha makes online quote sources easier to choose and quotes easier to get
(#317).

## Highlights

- **Choose a source from a list.** Online quotes lists the sources you can use
  here: BreadSched's own (`tsp`, `alphavantage`, `currency`) and every method your
  Finance::Quote offers, such as `usa` and `nyse` for US stocks and funds. A source
  that will not fetch, such as `vanguard`, is kept but you are told why. The same
  list is offered in the browser and by `breadsched quotes --list`.
- **Status you can see.** What Get quotes stored, or why it failed, now shows
  beside the buttons instead of out of view, and the dialog no longer stalls while
  opening or saving sources.
- **Quote a single security.** The Security price dialog has **Get online quote**,
  which fills in the current price for you to check before saving.

## Native-book compatibility

No schema change. Schema 11 books open directly; schemas 6 through 10 migrate
automatically with a verified backup. Quote sources already saved are unchanged.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. Going back to 0.2.0a278 needs no
restore.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes a
source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, Debian
and Fedora packages, and `SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
