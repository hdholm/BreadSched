# BreadSched 0.2.0a281

Application version: `0.2.0a281`

Native schema version: `11`

Supported native schemas: `6–11`

This alpha shows and takes shares in the register of an investment account that
holds a security (#323).

## Highlights

- **Shares in the register.** A fund or stock account, such as one imported from
  GnuCash, showed only dollar amounts. Its register in the desktop window and the
  browser now has **Shares** and **Price** for each transaction and **Shares Held**
  after each one, with a footer giving the shares held and their value at the
  latest price (with that price's date and source, or a plain "no quote").
- **Buying and selling from the register.** Type any two of shares, price, and
  amount and the third is filled in; the shares are saved with the transaction. A
  purchase or sale without shares is refused instead of being saved without them.
- **Shares in the transaction editor.** A split in such an account has a **Shares**
  entry beside its amount.

## Native-book compatibility

No schema change. Schema 11 books open directly; schemas 6 through 10 migrate
automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. Going back to 0.2.0a280 needs no
restore; shares entered with this version are ordinary split quantities.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes a
source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, Debian
and Fedora packages, and `SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
