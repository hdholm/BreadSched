# BreadSched 0.2.0a288

Application version: `0.2.0a288`

Native schema version: `12`

Supported native schemas: `6–12`

This alpha reorganizes how an account's type-specific settings are held (#339).
Nothing you see or store changes.

## Highlights

- **Settings grouped by account type.** A credit card's terms, a debt's interest and
  linked asset, an FSA's plan years, an investment's growth and cost-basis method,
  and the household's planning choices are each kept together, so the account
  editors and the checks made when an account is saved follow the account's type.

## Native-book compatibility

No schema change. Accounts are stored exactly as before; schema 12 books open
directly, and schemas 6 through 11 migrate automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. Going back to 0.2.0a287 needs no
restore.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes a
source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, Debian
and Fedora packages, and `SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
