# BreadSched 0.2.0a289

Application version: `0.2.0a289`

Native schema version: `12`

Supported native schemas: `6–12`

This alpha makes FSA claims and reimbursable expenses check their links to the
ledger the same way (#340).

## Highlights

- **A claim cannot take BreadSched's own reclassification.** When an expense is
  reimbursable, BreadSched keeps a transaction that moves what is owed into the
  receivable account. An FSA claim could link that transaction as if it were a
  provider refund. Saving such a claim is now refused with the same explanation the
  reimbursable expense gives, the claim is left as it was, and the claim screens no
  longer offer that transaction.
- **Internal simplification.** Claims and reimbursable expenses share one way of
  resolving, totalling, and refusing their links, so the two cannot drift apart.

## Native-book compatibility

No schema change. Schema 12 books open directly; schemas 6 through 11 migrate
automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. Going back to 0.2.0a288 needs no
restore.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes a
source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, Debian
and Fedora packages, and `SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
