# BreadSched 0.2.0a294

Application version: `0.2.0a294`

Native schema version: `12`

Supported native schemas: `6–12`

This alpha brings the desktop reconcile window much closer to GnuCash's (#362).

## Highlights

- **Starting balance first.** Before you start a statement, the window shows the
  account's reconciled balance on the statement date, so a difference made before
  this statement shows up straight away.
- **Add interest or a fee in one step.** A charge or credit on the statement that is
  not yet in the book is added cleared and starts checked.
- **Funds In and Funds Out.** Entries are listed in two columns, each with its
  checked total, under the starting, reconciled, and ending balances and the
  difference.
- **Check several at once.** Ctrl- or Shift-click rows, then choose **Check
  Selected** or **Uncheck Selected**.
- **Entries after the statement date** are listed below an *After* heading and
  shaded, in case a date is wrong. They are never checked for you. The browser
  marks them too.
- **Keep working while reconciling.** The window no longer blocks the rest of
  BreadSched, so you can enter a missed scheduled transaction or fix one in a
  register. The lists follow, and the window can enter or edit transactions itself.
- **Postpone or abandon.** Postpone keeps the statement to resume later. Abandon
  discards it without changing any transaction.

## Native-book compatibility

No schema change. Books open unchanged in either direction. Schema 12 books open
directly; schemas 6 through 11 migrate automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. Going back to 0.2.0a293 needs no
restore. A split reconciled from after its statement's date stays reconciled, but
0.2.0a293 counts it in the starting balance only of statements on or after its own
date.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes a
source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, Debian
and Fedora packages, and `SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
