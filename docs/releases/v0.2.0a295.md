# BreadSched 0.2.0a295

Application version: `0.2.0a295`

Native schema version: `12`

Supported native schemas: `6–12`

This alpha makes FSA claims easier to start, link, and find (#366).

## Highlights

- **Payments made with the FSA card count as reimbursed.** Link the healthcare
  payment on a claim and its FSA side is linked too, so the claim no longer shows the
  payment as still to reimburse. A refund back to the card likewise repays the FSA.
- **New means new.** The claims chooser starts with **New claim**. Saved claims show
  where they stand, and the chooser updates when a claim is saved or deleted.
- **Manageable transaction lists.** Linked transactions stay at the top, each with
  **Remove**. Below them you can search by date, description, or amount, and see
  transactions near the service date, in the claim year, or at any date.
- **Manage FSA Claims from anywhere.** It is in the Actions menu of every view. The
  window can stay open beside a register and picks up new transactions.
- **Every claim of the year.** The FSA Dashboard lists every claim of a benefit year
  that can still be claimed, settled or not, as well as older claims still open.

## Native-book compatibility

No schema change. Books open unchanged in either direction. Schema 12 books open
directly; schemas 6 through 11 migrate automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. Going back to 0.2.0a294 needs no
restore. Reimbursement links added when a claim was saved stay on the claim.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes a
source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, Debian
and Fedora packages, and `SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
