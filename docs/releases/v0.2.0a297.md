# BreadSched 0.2.0a297

Application version: `0.2.0a297`

Native schema version: `12`

Supported native schemas: `6–12`

This alpha makes entering split and everyday transactions quicker and clearer
(#369).

## Highlights

- **The rest on its own line.** While a split transaction does not balance, its
  empty last line shows the amount left; choose that line's account and it takes
  the amount, as in GnuCash. This works in the desktop and browser registers.
- **Tab to the right column.** After choosing an expense as the transfer, Tab goes
  straight to **Withdrawal** or **Charge**.
- **No more minus signs in the transaction editor.** Each split row has two amount
  columns named for its account, such as **Payment** and **Charge** for a card. A
  blank row shows what balances the rest, and an entry left out of balance gets a
  new row holding the difference, waiting for its account.

## Native-book compatibility

No schema change. Books open unchanged in either direction. Schema 12 books open
directly; schemas 6 through 11 migrate automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. Going back to 0.2.0a296 needs no
restore.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes a
source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, Debian
and Fedora packages, and `SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
