# BreadSched 0.2.0a299

Application version: `0.2.0a299`

Native schema version: `12`

Supported native schemas: `6–12`

This alpha tracks costs someone else will repay that you recorded straight to a
receivable account, and explains when to record a reimbursable cost which way
(#378).

## Highlights

- **Employer costs on your card.** A charge recorded to a Receivable account, such
  as Assets:Receivables:Employer, can be tracked as reimbursable. It gets a payer,
  an expected date, and a status, and never counts as your spending.
- **Paybacks on the paycheck.** A credit to that receivable account, such as a
  paycheck line, is proposed as the reimbursement and settles it.
- **Write-offs say where they go.** A write-off can name the expense it becomes,
  and must for a charge recorded to a receivable.
- **Two ways, explained.** The user guide's new section "Two ways to record a
  reimbursable cost" walks through both, with examples.

## Native-book compatibility

No schema change. A write-off that names an expense account stores it in the
receivable's record, which earlier versions ignore. Books open unchanged in either
direction. Schema 12 books open directly; schemas 6 through 11 migrate
automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. Going back to 0.2.0a298 needs no
restore. That version does not understand a charge recorded to a receivable as a
reimbursable cost, so leave such receivables alone there until you upgrade again.
