# BreadSched 0.2.0a304

Application version: `0.2.0a304`

Native schema version: `13`

Supported native schemas: `6–13`

This alpha adds a search across every register, with sorting, subtotals, and
totals (#382).

## Highlights

- **Find transactions.** It is **Edit → Find Transactions…** (Ctrl+Shift+F) on the
  desktop, the **Find** view in the browser, and `breadsched find` on the command
  line.
  - Text, an account and its subaccounts, dates, tags, an exact amount or a range in
    either direction, and money in or out can be combined.
  - An amount finds the leg of that size: 67 finds a 67.00 charge, never check 1067.
- **Reports from a search.** Rows sort by date, amount, account, or description,
  and group by account, month, year, or tag. Each group has a subtotal and the last
  line is the total, per currency.

## Native-book compatibility

No schema change. Books open unchanged in either direction. Schema 13 books open
directly; schemas 6 through 12 migrate automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. Going back to 0.2.0a303 needs no
restore.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes a
source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, Debian
and Fedora packages, and `SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
