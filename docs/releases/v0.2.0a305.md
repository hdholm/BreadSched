# BreadSched 0.2.0a305

Application version: `0.2.0a305`

Native schema version: `13`

Supported native schemas: `6–13`

This alpha starts faster and says when a book is opening (#389), lets desktop tabs
be reordered (#390), and sorts the reconciliation lists (#377).

## Highlights

- **Start-up.** The window appears at once with an **Opening** page naming the book
  until it is ready. A book with many missed scheduled dates no longer builds a
  drop-down for each before showing them: a schedule's dates open under **Show the
  dates**. Balances and the due list are computed in the database, exactly.
- **Reorder tabs.** Drag a tab onto another, or press Ctrl+Shift+Page Up/Down
  (**View → Move Tab Left/Right**). The order is remembered with the book's tabs.
- **Sort while reconciling.** Order Funds In and Funds Out by checked, date,
  description, or amount, either way, in the desktop window and the browser, to find
  an entry missing from a statement. Entries after the statement stay last.

## Native-book compatibility

No schema change. Books open unchanged in either direction. Schema 13 books open
directly; schemas 6 through 12 migrate automatically with a verified backup.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. Going back to 0.2.0a304 needs no
restore.

## Verified artifacts

After the complete CI matrix succeeds on main, the release workflow publishes a
source distribution, pure-Python wheel, Windows installer, Flatpak bundle, Debian
and Fedora packages, and `SHA256SUMS` for the tested commit.
