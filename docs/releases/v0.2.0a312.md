# BreadSched 0.2.0a312

Application version: `0.2.0a312`

Native schema version: `13`

Supported native schemas: `6–13`

This alpha begins decision 0005 (#397): an estimate is an allowance for its period,
and what it leaves lapses when the period ends.

## Highlights

- **Estimates lapse.** A groceries estimate of 600 for June that spent 550 no longer
  leaves 50 in its jar for July. The day after June's period ends, Budget jars show
  the 50 as **Lapsed**, and July starts with July's own 600. A period that spent more
  than its estimate is written off the same way, rather than owed by the next. A
  scheduled payment keeps its money until it is paid or skipped.
- **What to use when.** Decision 0005 now explains when a household should use a
  commitment, an estimate, a savings goal, or a budget jar, with worked examples.
  Money that should carry forward belongs in a goal, or in an estimate over the
  period it really covers.

## Command-line and browser compatibility

Budget jar periods gain `lapsed` (in `breadsched jars --json` and
`/api/budget-jars`), and the table gains a **lapsed** column before **level**.
An estimate's jar levels are lower than before wherever an earlier period left money.

## Native-book compatibility

No schema change. Nothing is stored: lapses are computed when the jars are read.

## Upgrade and rollback

Keep an independent backup of a real book. Going back to 0.2.0a311 needs no
restore; its jars carry estimate leftovers forward as before.
