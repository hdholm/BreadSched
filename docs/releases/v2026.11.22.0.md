# BreadSched 2026.11.22.0

The third beta of BreadSched, a household finance application for tracking your
bank and card accounts, seeing the cash you will have, budgeting with jars,
reporting on where the money goes, and planning years ahead. This beta is about
the desktop: commands are placed by one rule, Scheduled keeps your place, and a
desktop that stops responding now says so and can be quit and reported. The
website, <https://hdholm.github.io/BreadSched/>, walks through each step.

Application version: `2026.11.22.0`
Release stage: beta (a GitHub pre-release)
Native schema version: `14`
Supported native schemas: `13–14`

## What is new since 2026.11.18.0

Every change is on the desktop; the browser and the command line are unchanged.

- **Commands are where you expect them**, in every view, as in GnuCash and Gramps:
  - the menus list every command;
  - the toolbar shows the current view's, those on the selected row first
    (**Edit**, **Delete**, **Duplicate**, then the view's own), greyed out until a
    row is selected;
  - right-clicking a row (or Shift+F10, or the Menu key) lists the commands on it.

  Buttons inside a view now only change what it shows: pickers, filters, ranges
  and their **Apply**, and the register's **Enter**, **Cancel**, and **Split**.
  New commands: **Open Register** and **Delete Account…** in Accounts, and
  **Delete Transaction…** in the register (Ctrl+Z restores it).
- **Upcoming is now the first table in Scheduled**, above the definitions, with
  its look-ahead and overdue note, and **Review Due** is a Scheduled toolbar icon.
- **Scheduled keeps your place.** After you edit, enter, skip, match, or duplicate
  a schedule, it stays selected and in view. **Name** sorts again (#427).
- **The toolbar and the tab row scroll by arrows** at their ends when they are
  wider than the window, instead of a scrollbar under them; the mouse wheel
  scrolls them too, and choosing a tab brings it into view.
- **A desktop that stops responding says so.** After 20 seconds without a
  response (three minutes while a book opens or upgrades), a separate window asks
  whether to wait or quit, and where BreadSched was stuck is written to a hang
  log. After quitting, the next start offers to open a GitHub bug report with the
  start of the log filled in (nothing from your book). A dialog hidden behind
  another window now comes forward with the main window, and **View → Bring All
  Windows Forward** brings every window forward.
- **The row under the pointer stands out** from the shading of every other row.
- **Share prices has room to type**: the window opens as tall as its table needs,
  and the table always shows its heading and about three legs.

The full list is in the [changelog](https://github.com/hdholm/BreadSched/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Changes to what you do

- **Row commands moved off buttons.** Scheduled's **View / Edit…**, **Enter
  Next**, **Skip Next**, **Match…**, **Duplicate…**, and **Delete…**, Review's
  buttons, Plan's **Resolve actuals…**, **Add estimate…**, **Alter baseline…**,
  and **Suppress baseline…**, Projection's **Explain month…** and **Save Base as
  Scenario…** (now **Save Scenario**), and the FSA Dashboard's **Link selected** are
  toolbar icons and Actions menu items, and most are on the row's right-click
  menu. Each FSA claim's **Review claim** button is gone: select the claim and
  choose **Edit Claim**, or double-click it.
- **The separate Upcoming view is gone.** A book that had it open in a tab reopens
  Scheduled there.
- A register in a window of its own keeps **Edit**, **New**, **New Window**, and
  **Reconcile…** as buttons, since it has no toolbar.

## Upgrade and rollback

Books are unchanged (native schema 14), and this version opens every book the
earlier betas do: books written by 0.2.0a301 and later (native schemas 13 and 14).
A schema 13 book is brought forward to schema 14 when it is first opened for
writing, after a verified backup is written beside it. A book from an alpha before
0.2.0a301 is refused with a message saying so: open it once with 0.2.0a321, which
brings it forward, then open it here.

Installing over 2026.11.18.0, an earlier beta, or an alpha replaces the program and
keeps your books; uninstalling never removes a book. Back up your book before
upgrading (**File → Back Up Current Book…** or `breadsched backup`), and keep the
backup until you are satisfied.

To roll back, install 2026.11.18.0 again: it opens the same books, with its buttons
and its separate Upcoming view back. Hang logs in the data folder's `hang-reports`
are left alone and can be deleted.

## Verified artifacts

The release workflow builds every file from the tested commit of `main` and attaches:

- the wheel and source distribution;
- `BreadSched-2026.11.22.0-setup.exe`, the per-user Windows installer with its own
  Python and GTK runtime. It is built, installed over the previous beta's
  installer, exercised, and uninstalled on Windows;
- `BreadSched-2026.11.22.0.flatpak`, built and installed in its sandbox;
- `breadsched_2026.11.22.0_all.deb` and `breadsched-2026.11.22.0-1.noarch.rpm`,
  built and installed on their distributions;
- `SHA256SUMS` covering all of them.

**These files are not yet code-signed.** Windows SmartScreen may warn before the
installer runs. Check each download against `SHA256SUMS` before installing:

```bash
sha256sum --check --ignore-missing SHA256SUMS
```

On Windows PowerShell, compare `Get-FileHash .\BreadSched-2026.11.22.0-setup.exe`
with its line in `SHA256SUMS`.
