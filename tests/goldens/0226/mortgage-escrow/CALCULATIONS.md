# Mortgage and escrow calculations

The opening balance sheet is a $400,000 house, $10,000 cash, and $320,000 mortgage,
offset by $90,000 equity. Each $2,400 mortgage payment contains $800 principal,
$1,150 interest, and $450 escrow funding. The whole-payment row is non-additive context;
the bridge counts the three components once and changes cash by -$2,400.

After January, cash is $7,600, mortgage debt is $319,200, and house plus escrow holdings
are $400,450. In February another payment produces $318,400 debt and $900 escrow, then a
$900 property-tax payment consumes escrow without consuming spendable cash. Holdings
return to $400,000 and cash closes at $5,200. Both monthly ledgers conserve opening stock,
movements, and closing stock.
