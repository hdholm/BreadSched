# Plan cash timing calculations

Opening spendable cash on 2025-12-31 is $1,000. January's exact-dated plan is rent
-$500 on the 5th, a $100 card purchase on the 10th, and salary +$1,200 on the 20th.
Because the purchase uses credit, planned spendable cash changes by +$700 while operating
net is +$600. The minimum is $500 immediately after rent. February adds rent -$500,
salary +$1,200, another $100 card purchase, and settlement -$100, for +$600 cash and a
$2,300 close.

The matched salary posts two days late at $1,250, preserving its $1,200 expected amount:
amount variance +$50 and date variance +2 days. Unexpected groceries use $75 cash.
January actual cash is therefore $1,250 - $75 = $1,175. February is future at the as-of
date, so category variance is not a realized result. Month-to-quarter roll-up preserves
the $1,300 planned cash change and January 5 minimum.
