# Classified-flow calculations

Opening cash is $1,000 and opening loan principal is $500. Gross payroll is $1,200:
$900 cash, $200 retirement saving, and $100 FSA funding. A later $100 principal payment
uses cash, and a $50 retirement distribution restores cash while reducing the retirement
holding.

The four planning-purpose amounts are therefore retirement saving $200, benefit funding
$100, debt principal $100, and retirement distribution $50. Operating net remains the
$1,200 income statement amount. The non-overlapping cash bridge is
$1,200 - $200 - $100 - $100 + $50 = $850, closing cash at $1,850. Projection closes with
$250 in retirement/FSA holdings and $400 of debt; $1,850 + $250 - $400 = $1,700 net worth.
